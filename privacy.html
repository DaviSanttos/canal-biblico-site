Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Who uses this app

Canal Dark Bíblico is used only by its operator. It does not offer sign-up to the public and does not collect data from website visitors (no cookies, analytics or tracking on this site).

2. Data received from TikTok

When the operator authorizes the app on TikTok, it receives the operator's TikTok open ID, basic profile information (display name, avatar) and OAuth access/refresh tokens. These are used solely to upload videos as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account.

3. Storage and security

Tokens and data are stored only on the operator's own computer, never on third-party servers, and are never sold, shared or used for advertising.

4. Revoking access

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services), after which stored tokens are deleted.
