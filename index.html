Canal Dark Bíblico

A private, single-user studio that creates short videos about the Bible — verses, chapters, biblical stories and reflections — and sends them, after human review, to the operator's own social media accounts.

How it works

  1. Videos are produced from public-domain Bible texts, with narration, music and visuals assembled automatically.
  2. Every video is reviewed and approved by a human operator before anything is sent anywhere.
  3. Approved videos are uploaded, through the official TikTok Content Posting API, as drafts to the operator's own TikTok account. The operator finishes and publishes each post manually in the TikTok app.
